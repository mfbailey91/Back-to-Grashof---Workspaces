# R3A — L5 Five-Point Natural-Leaf Reconstruction Program

**Status:** Proposed active implementation program
**Project:** Back to Grashof — Mechanism-Based Workspace Characterization
**Rung:** L5 spatial 5R
**Primary target:** Pointing image in \(S^2\), not full \(SO(3)\) dexterity
**Program decision:** Establish one controlled, analytically checkable L5 reconstruction before returning to generic 5R architectures or beginning L6.

---

## 1. Program decision

The next software program will not attempt to rescue the rejected fixed-axis `UUUR` as an \(h(d)=c\) child, and it will not jump directly to a generic 6R orientation parent.

It will construct a positive-control spatial 5R arm whose pointing-complete workspace is known analytically, select five fixed Cartesian points spanning the positive region and both boundaries, and compare three independent representations of pointing capability:

```text
analytical pointing oracle
        +
independent source-chain target-direction IK
        versus
stitched source h=c fibers
        versus
stitched natural UURU mechanism leaves
```

The natural leaves are generated by fixing one coordinate of an exact virtual-spherical orientation chart. Once a child is instantiated, its geometry is frozen and its one-DOF closed-mechanism branch is continued without forcing the child to remain on the earlier \(h(d)=c\) pointing level set.

The program asks:

> At five fixed tool points of a controlled 5R arm, can a source-derived one-parameter family of exact `UURU` mechanism curves reconstruct the independently computed pointing image at declared spherical resolution?

## 2. What changed relative to V06D2

The current rejected result tested this claim:

```text
selected source h(d)=c fiber
        ==
seed-derived fixed-axis UUUR branch
```

The branch closed and lifted to fixed-position source configurations, but it did not remain on the selected \(h=c\) fiber and did not reproduce that source curve.

R3A tests a different claim:

```text
fixed virtual-spherical chart coordinate λ
        -> exact UURU child
        -> natural closed-mechanism branch C_λ
        -> union over λ
        -> source pointing image
```

The earlier result remains a valid rejection of one task-fiber equivalence. It is not treated as a rejection of every `UXXX` mechanism family.

## 3. Controlled 5R arm

### 3.1 Architecture

Use an idealized anthropomorphic arm:

```text
U_shoulder — R_elbow — U_wrist
```

Expanded as five revolute joints:

```text
R1(z at shoulder)
R2(y at shoulder)
R3(y at elbow)
R4(z at wrist center)
R5(y at wrist center)
```

The two shoulder axes intersect orthogonally. The two wrist axes intersect orthogonally. The elbow axis lies at the end of the first regional link. The tool point is offset from the wrist center along the selected tool-pointing axis.

The exact fixed-position closed source mechanism is role-aware:

```text
S_v — U_shoulder — R_elbow — U_wrist
```

or `SURU`, with generic mobility

\[
M = 3 + 2 + 1 + 2 - 6 = 2.
\]

Fixing one exact coordinate of \(S_v\) replaces the virtual spherical joint with a virtual universal joint and yields

```text
U_v — U_shoulder — R_elbow — U_wrist
```

or `UURU`, with

\[
M = 2 + 2 + 1 + 2 - 6 = 1.
\]

### 3.2 Normalized geometry

Use:

\[
L_1 = 1.0,\qquad
L_2 = 0.8,\qquad
\ell = 0.2.
\]

The wrist-center reachable shell is

\[
r_{\min}=|L_1-L_2|=0.2,
\qquad
r_{\max}=L_1+L_2=1.8.
\]

All joints are unrestricted periodic revolutes in the first program. Joint limits remain explicitly `not_modeled`.

## 4. Analytical pointing oracle

For a desired tool point \(p^*\) and pointing direction \(d\in S^2\), the wrist center must be

\[
w = p^* - \ell d.
\]

A direction is feasible exactly when

\[
r_{\min}\leq \|w\|\leq r_{\max}.
\]

Therefore the exact direction-level oracle is

\[
\boxed{
\text{feasible}(p^*,d)
\Longleftrightarrow
r_{\min}\leq \|p^* - \ell d\|\leq r_{\max}.
}
\]

Let \(\rho=\|p^*\|\). Since

\[
\|p^* - \ell d\|
\in
[|\rho-\ell|,\rho+\ell],
\]

the point is pointing-complete when

\[
\boxed{
|\rho-\ell|\geq r_{\min},
\qquad
\rho+\ell\leq r_{\max}.
}
\]

For the selected geometry, the robust non-singular pointing-complete annulus is

\[
0.4 < \rho < 1.6.
\]

The analytical oracle is used to validate the numerical source-chain reference and the final point classification. It is not used to accept a child mechanism or to fill missing numerical coverage cells silently.

## 6. The natural-leaf construction

### 6.1 Exact virtual-spherical chart

Represent the virtual spherical closure in a rotated Z–Y–Z chart:

\[
R_v(\alpha,\beta,\lambda)
=
C R_z(\alpha)R_y(\beta)R_z(\lambda)C^T R_{\mathrm{ref}},
\]

where \(C\) is a fixed chart basis and \(R_{\mathrm{ref}}\) is a fixed orientation reference.

For a fixed \(\lambda\),

\[
R_v(\alpha,\beta;\lambda)
=
C R_z(\alpha)R_y(\beta)
\underbrace{R_z(\lambda)C^T R_{\mathrm{ref}}}_{\text{fixed transform}},
\]

which is exactly a two-axis virtual universal joint followed by a fixed transform.

The child mechanism is therefore a genuine `UURU` loop with fixed geometry for the entire branch.

### 6.2 Meaning of “move as it pleases”

For one leaf:

1. choose a chart and a fixed coordinate \(\lambda_i\);
2. instantiate one independent `UURU` closed mechanism;
3. freeze the virtual-U axes, physical geometry, and fixed transform;
4. continue the one-DOF branch by pseudo-arclength;
5. do not enforce \(h(d)=c\) unless this is the separate source-fiber control;
6. do not recompute the child geometry at every continuation step.

A continuously relinearized or re-aggregated mechanism is not one four-bar and is outside the R3A mechanism-behavior path.

### 6.3 Family map

The desired family is

\[
\Phi(\lambda,s)=q_\lambda(s)\in\mathcal P_{p^*},
\]

where \(s\) is branch arclength. A local surface sweep requires

\[
\operatorname{rank}
\begin{bmatrix}
\partial\Phi/\partial s & \partial\Phi/\partial\lambda
\end{bmatrix}=2.
\]

R3A initially establishes a certified set cover, not a global fiber-bundle theorem.

## 7. Three independent reconstruction columns

### Column A — Independent direct source truth

For directions \(d_j\) on an icosphere, solve the original five-joint source problem:

\[
p(q)=p^*,
\qquad
d(q)=d_j.
\]

Use deterministic multistart nonlinear least squares, neighbor-seed propagation, wrapped-solution clustering, and explicit budgets.

Numerical outcomes are:

```text
FOUND
NOT_FOUND_AT_DECLARED_BUDGET
UNRESOLVED
```

No failed numerical solve is promoted to analytical unreachability. The analytical oracle is compared afterward.

### Column B — Source `h=c` stitching control

Use

\[
h(d)=n^T d=c
\]

as an explicit source-chain control family. Continue the constrained source branches directly without four-bar substitution. Paint and stitch their pointing curves onto the same sphere grid.

This column validates:

- seed discovery;
- 1D source continuation;
- component deduplication;
- curve-union coverage;
- refinement and metrics.

If this column fails, natural-child failure cannot yet be blamed on the decomposition.

### Column C — Natural `UURU` leaves

Use fixed-\(\lambda\) virtual-spherical chart leaves. Each accepted leaf must remain embedded in the original fixed-position source parent and satisfy the exact child/source closure and orientation map.

Only child components carrying `EXACT_GLOBAL` or `EXACT_ON_COMPONENT` closed-mechanism status contribute to the accepted-child reconstruction. Local or unresolved leaves may be plotted as diagnostics but do not count toward a workspace result.

## 8. Discovery and confirmation split

Do not use the same point-direction grid both to choose leaves and to claim reconstruction.

### Discovery data

May be used for:

- target-direction source solutions;
- chart assignment;
- circular \(\lambda\)-binning;
- seed selection;
- adaptive leaf insertion;
- duplicate detection.

### Confirmation data

Frozen before final comparison. Used only for:

- direct source confirmation;
- analytical-oracle comparison;
- source-fiber union metrics;
- natural-leaf union metrics;
- point-level pointing-complete classification.

The analytical oracle remains available for positive-control evaluation but must not silently repair the reconstructed set.

## 16. Definition of done

R3A is complete when the repository can answer, at each of the five fixed points:

1. What pointing directions are analytically feasible?
2. What directions are found by independent source-chain IK?
3. Can direct source `h=c` fibers reconstruct that set?
4. What exact `UURU` natural leaves are generated by fixed virtual-spherical coordinates?
5. Do those leaves remain embedded in the source parent?
6. Are they re-seed consistent and transverse as a family?
7. Does their accepted union reconstruct the source pointing image?
8. Does the resulting pointing-complete classification agree with the oracle?
9. Which gate failed when the answer is no?

---

## 17. What R3A does not claim

R3A does not establish:

- a universal 5R decomposition theorem;
- full \(SO(3)\) dexterity for a 5R arm;
- chart-independent child behavior at the individual-leaf level;
- a global foliation or fiber-bundle theorem unless all corresponding gates pass;
- that crank/rocker labels alone predict pointing completeness;
- a generic industrial-arm result;
- permission to replace exact child solves with the future numerical crank atlas.

The numerical mechanism atlas remains downstream of a successful source-to-child-to-parent reconstruction.
